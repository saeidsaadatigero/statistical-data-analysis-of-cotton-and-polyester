def eta_sq(groups):
    all_v = np.concatenate(groups)
    gm = all_v.mean()
    ss_b = sum(len(g) * (g.mean() - gm) ** 2 for g in groups)
    ss_t = ((all_v - gm) ** 2).sum()
    return ss_b / ss_t if ss_t else np.nan

Two findings worth your attention beyond the requested tests:
- The tension effect is entirely a polyester phenomenon. Split by fabric: polyester F(2,33) = 13.10, p < 0.001; cotton F(2,33) = 0.44, p = 0.65. Post-hoc (Bonferroni) localises it at the high setting only — low-vs-medium never differs. In cotton, fibre swelling swamps the stitch-tension contribution.
- Washes are repeated measures on the same specimens, so the requested one-way ANOVA treats paired data as independent (conservative). I ran it as asked and added a paired t-test: +1.03 ± 0.64 pp, t(35) = 9.59, p < 0.001 — all 36 specimens shrank further at wash 2.

Normality (Shapiro-Wilk) fails almost everywhere because shrinkage is recorded on a discrete 0.5 % grid, so I re-ran everything as Kruskal-Wallis. Every conclusion is reproduced — none depends on the normality assumption.
